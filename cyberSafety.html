Cyber Safety Tips for Students

In the digital age, protecting your personal data is just as important as protecting your physical belongings. Here are 5 essential rules to stay secure online.

1. Password Safety

Never use simple passwords like "123456" or your birthdate. Create strong passphrases using a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Always use different passwords for different accounts.

2. Phishing Awareness

Be highly suspicious of emails or texts asking for personal info urgently. Check the sender's actual email address, not just their name. When in doubt, do not click the link.

3. Safe Browsing

Ensure the websites you visit use "HTTPS" (look for the padlock icon in the address bar). Avoid downloading files from untrusted sites or using unsecured public Wi-Fi for banking.

4. Social Media Privacy

Review your privacy settings on platforms like Instagram and Facebook. Make your accounts private so only approved friends can see your posts, location, and personal details.

5. OTP & Scam Awareness

Your One-Time Password (OTP) is your final line of defense. Bank officials, customer service, or delivery agents will never ask for your OTP over a call. Never share it.
